Delete Your Momo Kids Account Momo Kids — published by MemorylineAI If you want to delete your Momo Kids account and the information associated with it, follow the steps below. There is no charge, and you do not need to give a reason. How to request deletion Send an email to subhasisj@memorylineai.com Use the subject line: Delete my Momo Kids account In the email, include the email address you used to sign in to Momo Kids. We need this to find your account. Please send the request from that same email address if you can, so we can confirm it is really you. We will confirm by email once the deletion is complete. We respond to every request within 30 days, and usually much sooner. What gets deleted When we delete your account, we remove: Your sign-in account (email address and password) Your name and your relationship to the child Your WhatsApp number, if you provided one Your child's first name and age Your city Your referral code and how you heard about us Your child's learning progress record What is kept, and for how long Nothing is kept once your account is deleted. We may keep basic records of the deletion request itself — the date and the fact that a request was made — for up to 90 days, so that we can show the request was properly handled. These records do not contain your child's information. Information stored on your device Some information, such as your child's progress and any profile photo you added, is stored on your own phone or tablet rather than on our servers. To remove that, uninstall the Momo Kids app. Uninstalling clears all locally stored information immediately. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account from our servers. To do both, uninstall the app and send us the email above. Questions If you are unsure about anything, email subhasisj@memorylineai.com and we will help. See also our privacy policy.
