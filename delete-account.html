Delete Your MemoryLineAI Kids Account

MemoryLineAI Kids — published by MemoryLineAI

If you want to delete your MemoryLineAI Kids account and the information associated with it, follow the steps below. There is no charge, and you do not need to give a reason.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to subhasisj@memorylineai.com
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my MemoryLineAI Kids account
  3. In the email, include the email address you used to sign in to MemoryLineAI Kids. We need this to find your account. Please send the request from that same email address if you can, so we can confirm it is really you.
  4. We will confirm by email once the deletion is complete.

We respond to every request within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

When we delete your account, we remove:

  • Your sign-in account (email address and password), held on Google Firebase

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept once your account is deleted.

We may keep basic records of the deletion request itself — the date and the fact that a request was made — for up to 90 days, so that we can show the request was properly handled. These records do not contain your child's information.

Information stored on your device

Everything else you entered, such as your child's name, age and progress, your mobile number, city and any profile photo, is stored only on your own phone or tablet. We never receive a copy.

To remove that, uninstall the MemoryLineAI Kids app. Uninstalling clears all locally stored information immediately.

Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account from our servers. To do both, uninstall the app and send us the email above.

Questions

If you are unsure about anything, email subhasisj@memorylineai.com and we will help.

See also our privacy policy.
