Privacy Policy — Momo Kids Last updated: 4 September 2026 Momo Kids is a pre-school learning app for children aged 2 to 5, published by MemorylineAI. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what we do with it. We have written it in plain language because it is meant to be read by parents. Who this app is for Momo Kids is designed for children aged 2 to 5, and is intended to be set up and managed by a parent or guardian. All account creation, personal information and settings sit behind a parent gate that a young child cannot pass. Children do not create accounts, enter personal information, or communicate with anyone through the app. What we collect When a parent creates an account, we ask for: Parent's email address — used to create and sign in to the account Parent's WhatsApp number — optional, used to send learning updates about the child Relationship to the child — for example, mother, father, guardian Child's first name — used to personalise what the child sees in the app Child's age — used to choose age-appropriate activities City — to understand where our users are How you heard about us — optional Referral code — optional, if a parent was invited by someone else The app also stores your child's progress through the learning activities — which letters, numbers, shapes and colours have been completed, and on what dates. If a parent chooses to add a profile photo, the photo stays on the device. We do not upload it or store a copy. What we do not collect We want to be specific about this, because it matters for a children's app: We do not collect your child's location We do not access the microphone or camera to record your child We do not collect any device identifier or advertising ID We do not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools We do not show advertising of any kind We do not allow your child to chat, post or communicate with anyone We do not collect your child's surname, date of birth, photograph or voice How we use the information We use it only to: Create and secure the parent's account Personalise the app for the child, so activities greet them by name and match their age Keep a record of the child's learning progress so a parent can see how they are doing Send learning updates to parents who have asked for them We do not use any of this information to build advertising profiles or to target your child in any way. Who we share it with We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not share it with data brokers. The only third party that handles your data is Google Firebase, which provides the secure sign-in and storage that the app runs on. Firebase acts on our behalf and does not use your data for its own purposes. You can read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. Where the information is stored Account information is stored securely on Google Firebase servers. Your child's progress and any profile photo are stored on your own device. How long we keep it We keep account information for as long as the account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your information from our systems. Your rights as a parent You can, at any time: See what information we hold about you and your child Correct anything that is wrong Delete your account and all associated information Withdraw consent for us to contact you To do any of these, email subhasisj@memorylineai.com and we will respond within 30 days. Full instructions are on our account deletion page. You can also remove all locally stored information at any time by uninstalling the app. Children's privacy Momo Kids is built to comply with Google Play's Families policy. We collect the minimum information needed to make the app work for a child, and only with a parent's knowledge and consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child. If you believe a child has provided us with information without a parent's involvement, please contact us and we will delete it. Changes to this policy If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell parents by email.
